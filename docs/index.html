Post one announcement to every community you run.

WA Poster runs on your own computer. Pick a saved message, tick your communities, check how it will look in the chat, and it posts to each one in turn.

    What it does

    Saves your routine

    Keep every reminder, poll and pitch in one list. Import them from a spreadsheet, or write them in the app.

    Shows the chat first

    The preview looks like a WhatsApp chat, with bold, links and image captions as members will see them.

    Sends at a human pace

    One community at a time with a random gap you set, and small variations so the copies are not identical.

    Stays with you

    Your messages, images and WhatsApp login stay in the project folder on your computer. There is no cloud account.
